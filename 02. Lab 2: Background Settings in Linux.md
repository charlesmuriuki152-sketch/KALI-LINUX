# Kali Linux XFCE Desktop Customization Lab

**Lab Type:** Independent Lab
**Difficulty:** Beginner
**Duration:** 30–45 minutes
**Reference Video:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pyuceN8IUME

## 1. Objective

Learn how to customize the Kali Linux XFCE desktop by exploring background settings, themes, notifications, and panel appearance.

## 2. Requirements

* Kali Linux with XFCE desktop environment.
* Access to the graphical desktop.
* A standard user account.

## 3. Lab Exercises

### Exercise 1: Desktop Background

1. Open **Settings → Desktop**.
2. Locate the Background tab.
3. Select a different wallpaper.
4. Explore the available wallpaper display styles.
5. Examine the background color options.
6. Restore your preferred settings.

**Expected Result:** You can change the wallpaper and adjust its display settings.

### Exercise 2: Appearance and Themes

1. Open **Settings → Appearance**.
2. Explore the available themes.
3. Select a theme and observe the changes.
4. Open **Settings → Window Manager**.
5. Examine the available window decoration themes.

**Expected Result:** You understand how themes affect application appearance and window decorations.

### Exercise 3: Notification Settings

1. Open **Settings → Notifications**.
2. Examine the available notification options.
3. Identify controls related to notification appearance and behavior.
4. Record your observations.

**Expected Result:** You can locate and identify notification settings available on your system.

### Exercise 4: Panel Settings

1. Open **Settings → Panel**.
2. Explore the panel display options.
3. Open the Appearance section.
4. Examine background styles, opacity, and icon sizing where available.
5. Restore any settings you changed.

**Expected Result:** You can identify the main panel customization options.

## 4. Review Questions

1. How do you change the desktop wallpaper?
2. What is the purpose of wallpaper display styles?
3. How do appearance themes affect the desktop?
4. What can you configure in notification settings?
5. Which appearance options are available in Panel Preferences?

## 5. Completion Checklist

* [ ] Changed the desktop wallpaper.
* [ ] Explored wallpaper display styles.
* [ ] Examined appearance and window themes.
* [ ] Inspected notification settings.
* [ ] Explored panel appearance options.
* [ ] Restored unwanted changes.
