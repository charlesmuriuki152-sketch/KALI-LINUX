**Lab Type:** Independent Practical Lab
**Difficulty:** Beginner
**Estimated Duration:** 30–45 minutes
**Environment:** Kali Linux with XFCE Desktop

---

## 1. Objective

Explore and customize the Kali Linux XFCE desktop environment by working with desktop backgrounds, appearance themes, notification settings, and panel preferences.

## 2. Requirements

* Kali Linux with the XFCE desktop environment.
* Access to the graphical desktop.
* A standard user account.

## 3. Practical Exercises

### Exercise 1: Desktop Background

1. Open **Settings → Desktop**.
2. Locate the background settings.
3. Select a different wallpaper.
4. Explore the available wallpaper display styles.
5. Examine the background color options.
6. Restore your preferred settings if necessary.

**Expected Outcome:** Successfully change the desktop wallpaper and identify the available display options.

### Exercise 2: Appearance and Themes

1. Open **Settings → Appearance**.
2. Explore the available themes.
3. Select an alternative theme and observe the changes.
4. Open **Settings → Window Manager**.
5. Examine the available window decoration themes.

**Expected Outcome:** Understand how appearance themes and window decorations affect the desktop.

### Exercise 3: Notification Settings

1. Open **Settings → Notifications**.
2. Examine the available notification options.
3. Identify settings related to notification appearance and behavior.
4. Record your observations.

**Expected Outcome:** Locate the notification configuration interface and identify its available controls.

### Exercise 4: Panel Settings

1. Open **Settings → Panel** or access **Panel Preferences** from the panel's context menu.
2. Explore the available display settings.
3. Open the **Appearance** section.
4. Examine background styles, opacity, and icon sizing where available.
5. Restore any settings you do not want to keep.

**Expected Outcome:** Identify the main panel display and appearance options.

---

## 4. Review Questions

1. How do you change the desktop wallpaper in Kali Linux?
2. What is the purpose of wallpaper display styles?
3. How do appearance themes differ from window decoration themes?
4. What types of settings can be configured in the notification interface?
5. Which appearance options are available in Panel Preferences?
